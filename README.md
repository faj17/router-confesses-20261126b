One line a home Wi-Fi router gives for dropping the connection at the exact
moment a movie reaches its climax:

> I know who did it, and after three years of no thank-you, you'll have to guess.

The router is not apologizing for a glitch — it is holding the ending hostage.
It already knows who the killer is, and after three years of blinking away
unnoticed in the corner with never a single thank-you, it has decided the
viewer can guess. A petty appliance with a grudge and a smug little secret,
no buffering, no bandwidth, no firmware update in sight.
